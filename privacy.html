Brilliant & Overlooked: privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who uses it

The Brilliant & Overlooked publishing tool is private. Only the owner of the YouTube channel Brilliant & Overlooked uses it, on his own computer, for his own channel. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos he has approved to his own channel, manage his own playlists, and read his own channel's information and analytics.

What it stores

The sign-in token and the channel statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, or sent anywhere except to YouTube itself.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Through the channel: youtube.com/@BrilliantOverlooked.
